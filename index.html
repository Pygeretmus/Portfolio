How I take a project from start to launch, the way I did at DriveSmart.

DriveSmart

A car-sharing app in Dubai where people rent cars from private owners. Owners list their cars, renters book and pay in the app, and the platform holds the money until the car is returned. I built the whole server side of the app from scratch.

Company
Zyklum (Germany, remote)
When
Jul 2025 – Jun 2026
Role
Backend developer
Stack
Django, DRF, PostgreSQL, Celery, Redis, MamoPay, AWS
  • Bookings from request to return

    Every rental moves through clear steps: request, owner approval, car handover, return. The system knows what is allowed at each step, so a booking can never end up in a broken state.

  • Payments and payouts

    Renters pay by card in the app, and owners get paid automatically after the rental. Extensions, early or late returns, car delivery, damage and fines are all charged correctly.

  • Fair cancellations

    The earlier a renter cancels, the more money they get back; owners who cancel pay a penalty. Everything is calculated automatically.

  • Cloud setup and releases

    Set up the servers on Amazon Web Services. Every change is tested automatically and released without manual steps.

  • Sign-in, chat and tools for the team

    Sign-in with Google or a phone number, chat between owners and renters, push notifications, and an admin panel where the team checks documents and resolves disputes.
